Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ScanSeq (jeonghopark.github.io/scanseqjs) handles your data when you use this application.

No Data Collection

ScanSeq does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. This application runs entirely in your web browser. No information about you or your usage is sent to any server.

Cookies

ScanSeq does not use cookies of any kind.

Camera Data

ScanSeq requests access to your device's camera in order to function. Your camera feed is processed exclusively within your browser in real time. No images, video frames, or any data derived from your camera are stored locally or transmitted to any external server.

Third-Party Services

ScanSeq does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Contact
